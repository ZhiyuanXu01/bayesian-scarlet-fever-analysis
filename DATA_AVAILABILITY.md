# Data Availability

Individual-level scarlet fever records used in this study are not distributed in this public repository.

The restricted analytical data contain sensitive health, temporal, and geographic information, including combinations of individual characteristics, onset or diagnosis dates, residential locations, and coordinates. Public release could create disclosure risks and may conflict with the conditions under which the records were obtained.

Accordingly, this repository excludes:

- individual-level case and control-day records;
- detailed residential addresses and individual coordinates;
- onset and diagnosis dates linked to individuals;
- individual identifiers and row-level model outputs;
- intermediate linked datasets derived from the restricted records.

Meteorological, air-pollution, population, and geographic source data originated from external providers and remain subject to their respective access conditions and licences. They are not redistributed here.

The public repository documents the expected analytical-data schema, processing workflow, statistical methods, and selected aggregate results. Access to the original health records cannot be granted through this repository and would require authorization from the relevant data custodians.
